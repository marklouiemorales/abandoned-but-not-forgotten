# PROJECT OVERVIEW:

Project 'Remember Me': The Untold Story of the Abandoned is an immersive, web-based
interactive storytelling on a game-approach experience designed to bring forgotten space
history closer to the next generation.

# WHAT IT DOES

• Gamified Storytelling Approach: Merges narrative exploration with game mechanics,
allowing users to take on the role of a Space Commander who actively uncovers
first-person historical accounts from personified space hardware rather than reading
passive text.
• Immersive Terminal Interface: Greets users with an advanced space station terminal
where they enter their name and receive formal mission briefings from NASA.
• Interactive Lunar & Martian Expeditions: Allows users to scan celestial surfaces using
simulated Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and orbital telemetry to locate, inspect, and
archive space equipment.
• Reward & Certification System: Awards personalized lunar and Martian pioneer
certificates, crowning the user as an Ultimate Space History Keeper upon mission
completion.

# HOW IT WORKS

• Launch & Initialization: The user opens the web application, clicks 'START JOURNEY',
enters their astronaut name, and accepts their first lunar mission.
• The Moon Mission: Traveling aboard their spaceship past atmospheric layers, the user
lands in the Sea of Tranquility. Utilizing a wrist radar synced with the Lunar
Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO), they locate three landmark equipment pieces: the Apollo
11 Lunar Module Eagle, Surveyor 3, and the Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle. Clicking
each piece triggers first-person audio-visual storytelling where the equipment shares its
historical journey.
• Transition to Mars: Following a successful lunar archive, Mission Control dispatches an
urgent secondary directive to Mars.
• The Mars Mission: Using orbital telemetry linked to the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter
(MRO) and SAR scanning, the user discovers three iconic Martian artifacts: NEAR
shoemaker, the Ingenuity Helicopter, and the InSight Lander, unlocking their historical
accounts.
• Debrief & Certification: The user blasts off to space for a continuation of their journey,
receives a certificate for successfully completing the mission and earns the title,
"Ultimate Space History Keeper".

# CORE BENEFITS

• Active Engagement: Replaces passive textbook reading and static digital scroll-down
approach of storytelling with exploratory gamification tailored for young learners.
• Emotional Connection: Personifies hardware artifacts so students build empathy and
curiosity toward the machines that made human space exploration possible.
• High Accessibility: Operates directly in standard web browsers with intuitive UI
controls, zero login barriers, and full mobile/desktop compatibility.

# NASA DATA

We leveraged a wide array of official NASA datasets, mission archives, and telemetry resources
to ground our narrative in absolute scientific accuracy:
• NASA 3D Resources & Asset Integration: The availability of official NASA 3D models
for equipment such as the Apollo Lunar Modules and Mars rovers directly inspired our
decision to build an interactive 3D experience, allowing us to bring these assets to life
via Blender 5.1 and Three.js.
• NSSDCA (NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive): Referenced for historical
launch dates, vehicle structures (Saturn V, Atlas-Centaur, Delta II, Atlas V), manufacturer
backgrounds (Grumman, Hughes, Boeing, Lockheed Martin), and mission outcomes.
• NASA Solar System Exploration & Mission Profiles: Direct technical parameters and
operational lifespans for Apollo 11, Surveyor 3, Apollo 15 LRV, Mars NEAR Shoemaker,
Ingenuity, and InSight.

# HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY

• What we built: We developed Project 'Remember Me', an immersive, web-based
interactive storytelling and exploration experience featuring a futuristic archival terminal
theme. It immerses users as "Space Commanders" investigating historic hardware left
behind on the Moon and Mars for them to experience storytelling through a unique game
approach, allowing them to actively uncover first-person accounts from personified
space hardware rather than reading passive text.
• How it addresses the challenge: Rather than relying on static scroll-down narration
facts to storytell, the platform leverages gamification, immersive narration, and
interactive 3D models (custom-modeled in Blender 5.1 and powered by Three.js) to let
young learners actively discover historical equipment-such as the Apollo 11 Lunar
Module Descent Stage, Surveyor 3, the Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle, the Sojourner
Rover, the Ingenuity Helicopter, and the InSight Lander. Users navigate a complete
space journey from Earth, manage an emergency Martian dust storm via manual
override thrusters, and archive equipment histories to earn an official Space History
Keeper certificate.
• Why it matters: Educational research demonstrates that game-based learning
significantly boosts behavioral, emotional, and cognitive engagement among
grade-school students (ages 8-12). By transforming dry historical data into an active
discovery adventure, Project Remember Me bridges the gap between complex space
science and young learners, inspiring the next generation of space explorers, scientists,
and engineers to ensure no legacy is left behind.
